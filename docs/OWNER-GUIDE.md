# JFL Solar website — owner and staff guide

This guide explains how to update the website yourself. You do not need to edit
any code.

## Signing in

1. Go to **jflsolar.com/admin** (or the preview address you were given).
2. Choose **Continue with Google** and pick your work Google account.
3. If you see "You don't have staff access", an owner must first add your exact
   Google email in **Admin → Staff**.
4. Use **Sign out** (bottom of the menu) on shared computers. If a save says your
   session expired, sign in again in a new tab, then press Save again — your
   typing is kept.

## How publishing works

Every package, product, project and category is in one of three states:

| State | Public website | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Hidden | You are still preparing it |
| **Published** | Visible | It is ready for customers |
| **Archived** | Hidden | It is no longer offered but you want to keep it |

- New items always start as **drafts**. Use **Save and publish** when ready.
- Editing a **published** item changes the public page as soon as you save. To
  work on it privately, choose **Save and unpublish** first.
- **Archive** instead of deleting. Only archived items can be **deleted
  permanently**, and you will be asked to confirm. Deleting removes its photos too.
- If two people edit the same item at once, the second save is refused with a
  message so nobody's work is silently overwritten — reload and re-apply.
- The **↑ ↓** buttons set the order on the website. **★** features an item on
  the home page.
- Changes appear on the website within about a minute.

## "Sample" items and "Awaiting confirmation"

The proposal includes demonstration content so the design can be judged:

- Items marked **Sample** are examples, not real JFL offers or installations.
  They are labelled on the preview site and **never shown on the live site**.
  Replace the text and photos, then untick **Sample / demonstration content**.
- Text in **Site content** marked **Awaiting confirmation** (founding date,
  founder profile, "Tier-1", replacement/loaner units, free technical support,
  after-sales) is shown only on the preview site. Once the facts are approved,
  edit the block and tick **The owner has confirmed this text is accurate**.
- The **Overview** page lists everything still to replace before launch.

## Packages

- **Offer type**: *Installed system* (supply + installation) or *Equipment only*
  (no installation). This is shown prominently so customers can't confuse them.
- **Capacity**: inverter kW, solar array kWp, battery kWh. Leave blank if sized
  after assessment.
- **Components**: one per line — `Component | details | quantity`.
- **Included / Not included**: one item per line.
- **Pricing mode**: *Request a quotation* (no price), *Fixed price*, or *Starting
  price* ("From ₱…"). Add a **price basis** such as "Pickup price, equipment only".
  Tick **sample price** for any price that is not an approved offer.
- **Promotion**: label and start/end dates (Philippine time, inclusive). After the
  end date the promotional price disappears automatically and customers see
  "Promotion ended — request current price". Tick **Hide this package after the
  promotion ends** to remove it from the site instead.
- **Warranty terms**: only enter confirmed terms. Leave blank to show the general
  warranty text from Settings.

## Products and categories

- Choose a **category**, add **brand**, **model**, and **specifications** (one per
  line, `Label: value`). Only enter specifications you have confirmed.
- **Availability** is a general status, not a stock count.
- Upload the manufacturer's **datasheet** (PDF, max 4 MB) on the product page.
- Listing a brand does not mean JFL Solar is an authorised distributor — don't
  claim that in descriptions unless it is confirmed.
- **Categories** drive the product filters. A category that still has products
  cannot be deleted.

## Projects and photos

- Use only **real JFL Solar projects** with photos you have permission to publish.
  Never use stock photos or other companies' work.
- Give the **general location** (city or province), never a client's address.
- Tick **System size is confirmed** only when the kWp figure is verified.
- **Photos**: JPEG, PNG or WebP. Large phone photos are reduced automatically.
  Every photo needs **alternative text** — a short description for people who
  can't see it (e.g. "Workers installing panels on a warehouse roof").
- Use **Make cover photo**, **↑ Earlier / ↓ Later**, **Replace file** and
  **Delete** under each photo. Photos on drafts stay private.

## Site content (About page)

Company introduction, story, mission, vision, founder profile and the four "Why
choose us" points. Separate paragraphs with a blank line. Untick **Show on the
website** to hide a block temporarily. The founder block also accepts a photo.

## Contact and settings

- Phone numbers have two forms: how it **looks** ("0969 471 2098") and the
  **international** form used for tap-to-call ("+639694712098").
- Turn on **WhatsApp**, **Viber**, **Facebook** and **Messenger** buttons only
  after checking they work. The Facebook link is currently **off** because the
  page could not be opened publicly when the site was built.
- Office and warehouse addresses are shown separately.
- **General warranty information** appears wherever a package or product has no
  specific warranty terms.
- The address that **receives inquiry emails** is not here — it is set by your
  developer for security.

## Staff (owners only)

Add a person's Google email and choose **Editor** (content) or **Owner** (content
and staff). Deactivate or remove people who leave. The last owner cannot be
removed.

## Inquiries

Customer inquiries arrive by email at the business inbox. Reply directly — the
customer's email is set as the reply-to address. The website does not store
inquiries itself. If the inbox stops receiving inquiries, ask your developer to
check the email service (Resend) dashboard.
